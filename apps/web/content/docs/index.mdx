title: Introduction

Type what you spent or received in plain words, like `180 iced coffee`, and Goatside
turns it into a categorized transaction. On your phone it's a quick logger and a chat
with its agent. On a wide screen it's a dashboard for reviewing your money, with charts
and a full transactions table.

## How it works

1. **You write one line.** `180 iced coffee`, `+18000 client payment`,
   `2400 dinner with nini and daniel split 3`. Code reads the amount exactly as you
   typed it. No model ever changes a number.
2. **The agent files it.** It picks the category, notices bills that repeat, spots
   people you lent to or split with, and works out which account the money moved in.
3. **It asks when it isn't sure.** A small question card shows its best guess, and one
   tap confirms or fixes it. Every answer teaches the next guess.
4. **You ask it things.** "How much on food this month?" gets an answer from your own
   ledger. It can also re-file, log or delete entries for you, after you confirm.

## Built for one person

There's no Goatside service to sign up for. Each copy belongs to one person, who
deploys it to their own Vercel account with their own database. It has exactly one
account, and you set its username and password yourself.

What you need:

- A [Vercel](https://vercel.com) account, for hosting and AI Gateway.
- A [Turso](https://turso.tech) database, for your ledger.
- About 15 minutes.

Hosting and the database fit in the free tiers for one person. AI usage for hundreds of
entries a month should come to well under a dollar.
