Goatside can keep track of where your money is: banks, e-wallets, credit cards and cash,
each with its balance. Accounts are optional. If you don't add any, entries have no
account.

## Setting them up

Add accounts on the **Accounts** page, or tell the agent: "add my BPI savings with
₱15,000". The first account you add becomes your **default**, where entries that don't
name an account go. You can change the default on the Accounts page.

A credit card shows what you owe and the credit left. When your limit changes, open the
card on the **Accounts** page and choose **Edit**. The same form changes an account's
name, color, last four digits and nicknames. Your entries stay as they are.

## Naming the account in a line

```txt
200 taxi via gcash
1500 groceries using bpi card
89 spotify
```

Say which account you used after _via_, _using_, _thru_, _gamit_, _paid with_ or
_charged to_, among others. Goatside matches accounts by name or nickname, so `bpi card`
and `bpi` can be different accounts. _Cash_ and _credit card_ mean your only account of that
kind.

Bills and subscriptions go on the account they were charged to last time, so `89 spotify`
lands on the same card each month.

A name you haven't set up, like `using unionbank`, becomes a question. You can add it as a
bank, e-wallet or card, or say it isn't an account. Goatside recognizes well-known
Philippine banks and e-wallets without a cue word, such as GCash, BPI, BDO, UnionBank,
GoTyme and SeaBank.

## Transfers

Moving money between your own accounts is a transfer, not spending:

```txt
1000 cash in gcash from bpi
3000 atm
5000 pay bpi card
2000 transfer from bdo to gotyme
```

Transfer words include _cash in_, _top up_, _ATM_, _withdraw_, _cash out_, _card payment_,
_transfer_, _instapay_ and _deposit_. A fee, like `15 atm fee`, is spending. When a line could
go either way, the agent asks.

Transfers never count in your totals or charts.

## Balances

A balance is where the account started plus everything filed to it, so it's never out
of sync with your entries. When it doesn't match your bank, use **Correct the balance**
on the account, or tell the agent "my Maya actually has ₱2,000". Your entries stay as
they are.

Archive an account you've closed to hide it without losing its history.

Behind on logging? [Import the statement](/docs/guide/importing) your bank or card sends
you, and Goatside adds the rows you didn't type.
