---
title: People and IOUs
description: Loans, repayments and split bills, and who owes whom.
---

Mention someone in a line and Goatside keeps track of what's owed. The home screen and
**People** show where you stand with everyone, with one-tap **Settle**.

```txt
1000 lent nini
2400 dinner with nini and daniel split 3
+500 daniel paid me back
350 lunch, daniel paid
```

The agent works out what kind of line it is:

| Kind             | Example                                    | What it records                                          |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| You lent         | `1000 lent nini`                           | Nini owes you ₱1,000                                     |
| You borrowed     | `350 lunch, daniel paid`                   | You owe Daniel ₱350                                      |
| Paid back to you | `+500 daniel paid me back`                 | Daniel owes you ₱500 less                                |
| You paid back    | `1000 paid back nini`                      | You owe Nini ₱1,000 less                                 |
| Shared           | `2400 dinner with nini and daniel split 3` | ₱800 is your spending, and Nini and Daniel owe ₱800 each |

IOUs never count as spending or income. Only your own share does. When a line could be
either your own spending or an IOU, the agent asks, and **Just my spending** is always
an option.

Goatside looks for names after words like _with_, _by_, _from_, _to_, _for_ and _lent_. It
recognizes anyone you've logged before anywhere in a line.

## On the People page

- Each person's balance and history.
- **Settle** all or part of a balance.
- **Nicknames**, so "nins" and "Nini" are the same person, and **merge** two people
  who turned out to be one.
- **Add an IOU** by hand: who, which way, how much and what for.
- **Draft a reminder.** The agent writes a short, friendly payment reminder from their
  open IOUs. Edit it, then copy or share it yourself. Goatside never sends it for you.

## Editing a split

Open a split bill in the edit drawer to change who it was split with, the number of
ways or the total. Goatside divides the shares evenly again, and everyone's rows move
together when you change the date, description or account.
