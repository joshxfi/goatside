---
title: Asking the agent
description: Questions about your money, and changes made for you after you confirm.
---

Type a question in the composer, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd> to open **Ask
Goatside**. Anything that doesn't start with an amount, or ends in `?`, is a question.

```txt
how much on food this month?
what were my biggest expenses last week?
sino may utang sakin?
how much is left on my credit card?
```

Every figure in an answer comes from your ledger, through fixed, read-only lookups. The
agent never writes its own database queries.

## Asking it to do things

The agent can also make changes:

- "move all grab rides this month to Transport"
- "delete the duplicate lunch"
- "log 350 lunch yesterday"
- "Daniel paid me back 500"
- "add my Security Bank savings with ₱15,000"
- "my Maya actually has ₱2,000"

Nothing changes until you confirm. First, a card lists the exact entries that will
change. The server builds that list from your ledger, so it isn't the model's own
description. Approve or decline it. You can undo any change afterwards.

## What it can't do

- Answer without a connection, or without AI configured.
- Delete more than 50 entries in one go.
- See anything outside your own ledger.

The chat model is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash by default. Goatside sends it your question and the
rows it looks up, through your AI Gateway. See [What leaves your instance](/docs/guide/privacy).
