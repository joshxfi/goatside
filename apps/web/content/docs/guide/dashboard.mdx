On a screen 1024px or wider, Goatside becomes a place to review rather than log. A
sidebar holds **Overview**, **Transactions**, **Accounts**, **People**, **Settings** and
**Ask Goatside** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>J</kbd>). The composer moves to a single line in the
header (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>). On Windows and Linux, use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> for <kbd>⌘</kbd>.

## Overview

The month at a glance, from top to bottom:

- **Headline numbers**: money in, money out and what's left.
- **Your accounts**, each with its balance.
- **Pace**: this month against last month, with a projection to the month's end.
  Repeating bills count once; everything else runs at your everyday daily rate.
- **Where it went**: a donut of your top categories, with the rest folded into Other.
- **Day by day** beside a calendar heatmap, stronger on bigger days.
- **Six months** stacked by category, and money in and out per month.
- **Biggest changes** per category against the same point last month, your largest
  expenses, and recent entries.
- **People** who owe you or whom you owe, and your **repeats**.

IOUs and transfers between your own accounts never count as spending.

## Transactions

The full ledger in a table:

- A period picker: any month, the last 3 months, this year, or all time.
- Search, plus filters for category, account and type (out, in, IOUs, transfers).
- Sorting on any column.
- Select rows (<kbd>Shift</kbd>-click for a range) to move them to a category or an
  account, or delete them with Undo.
- **Export** exactly what's filtered as a CSV.

Click any row to open it in the edit drawer.

## Editing an entry

The edit drawer covers everything the agent decides, so no fix needs a model or a
retyped entry. You can change the amount, date, description, type (spent, received,
transfer, lent, borrowed), category, account, who it was split with and whether it
repeats every month.

Every change you make is remembered as a correction, so the agent files the next
similar entry your way.
