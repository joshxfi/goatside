---
title: On your phone
description: Install Goatside on your home screen, and log entries with no signal.
---

On a phone, Goatside is a logger first. It shows your month's summary, daily spending,
people and recent entries, with the composer pinned where your thumb is.

## Getting around

A tab bar at the bottom opens **Overview**, **Transactions**, **Accounts** and
**People**. Tap the tab you're on to jump back to the top. **Settings**, **Ask
Goatside** and **Sign out** are in the menu behind your initial, top right. The tab bar
steps aside while you type.

In the installed app, pull down at the top of any page to reload it.

## Install it

Goatside is an installable web app. Open it in your browser and go to
**Settings → Home screen**:

- On Android and desktop Chrome, tap **Install**.
- On iPhone and iPad, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Settings shows these
  steps too.

It then opens full screen from its own icon, like any other app.

## Offline

- **Opening.** The four tab pages, and any page you've opened before, load from your
  phone when there's no signal, so your overview is there on a plane. They show what
  was last loaded. A page you've never opened says you're offline.
- **Logging.** Goatside saves every entry on your phone first, then sends it. With no signal it
  shows in your feed as **Waiting to sync**, and goes out by itself when you're back
  online. Tap a waiting entry to remove it. A dropped connection or a reload can't lose
  it, and an entry typed "yesterday" keeps its meaning even if it syncs days later.
- **Asking** the agent needs a connection.

## Updates

The installed app checks for a new version when you come back to it. When there is
one, it loads the next time you return after a while away, never while you're in the
middle of something.

Signing out clears the pages kept on your phone, even with no signal. So does a sign-in
that has expired: the next time Goatside opens online, they're deleted, and pages older
than 30 days never open offline.
