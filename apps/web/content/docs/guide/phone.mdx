On a phone, Goatside is a logger first: your month's summary, daily spending, people
and recent entries, with the composer pinned where your thumb is.

## Install it

Goatside is an installable web app. Open it in your browser and go to
**Settings → Home screen**:

- On Android and desktop Chrome, tap **Install**.
- On iPhone and iPad, follow the steps shown there: **Share**, then
  **Add to Home Screen**.

It then opens full screen from its own icon, like any other app.

## Offline

- **Opening.** Pages you've opened before load from your phone when there's no
  signal, so your overview is there on a plane. A page you've never opened says
  you're offline.
- **Logging.** Every entry is saved on your phone first, then sent. With no signal it
  shows in your feed as **Waiting to sync**, and goes out by itself when you're back
  online. Tap a waiting entry to remove it. A dropped connection or a reload can't lose
  it, and an entry typed "yesterday" keeps its meaning even if it syncs days later.
- **Asking** the agent needs a connection.

Signing out clears the pages kept on your phone.
