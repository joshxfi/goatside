---
title: When the agent asks
description: How the agent decides, when it asks you instead, and how it learns from you.
---

The agent only files an entry on its own when it's confident. When it isn't, it saves
the entry with its best guess and asks you, one card at a time above the composer. Entries
with an open question show a **Check** marker in your feed.

The questions you'll see:

- **Money in or out?** "₱500 for 'lunch': was that money out or in?" for an entry
  without a sign that it's torn on.
- **Which category?** Its top three guesses, with **Other…** to pick any category.
- **Who's involved?** Whether a line was your own spending, a loan, or a shared bill.
- **Is this a duplicate?** **Keep both** or **Remove this one**, when the same payment
  looks logged twice.
- **Which account?** When a line names an account you haven't set up, like
  `500 groceries using unionbank`: add it as a bank, e-wallet or card, or say it isn't
  an account.
- **Transfer or spending?** When a line could be moving money between your own
  accounts.

One tap answers. **Keep guess** confirms what's already there.

## How it learns

Every answer, and every fix you make in the edit drawer, is remembered. The next time you
log the same thing, Goatside files it the way you did, often without asking the model at
all. Categories also learn from the entries you've filed in them, so a category of your
own starts catching the right entries after a few uses.

Bills that come back month after month are marked as **repeating**, which the pace
projection and the Repeats card use.

## Without AI

With no AI configured, nothing is categorized for you, but a line without a sign still
gets a "money out or in?" card.
