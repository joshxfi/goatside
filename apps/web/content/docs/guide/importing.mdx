---
title: Importing statements
description: Add a bank or card statement's rows without typing them, or typing them twice.
---

When you've fallen behind on logging, import the statement your bank or card sends you.
Goatside adds the rows you didn't log, links the ones you did, and saves nothing until
you've reviewed them.

## Importing one

1. Open the account on the **Accounts** page and choose **Choose a statement**.
2. Pick the PDF your bank or card sends you, or a CSV export.
3. If the PDF has a password, type it. Banks often use your birthday or part of your
   account number.
4. Review the rows and choose **Import**.

Each statement goes into the account you opened it from. If the account has its last four
digits set and the statement never mentions them, Goatside warns you before you import.

## The file stays on your device

Your browser opens the file and reads it. The PDF and its password never reach your
server or any AI model, and Goatside doesn't keep them. What gets saved is each row's
date, details and amount, the balances the statement printed, and a fingerprint of the
file so you can see if you import it again.

## Every row has to add up

Goatside checks the rows against the statement before it shows them to you:

- Each running balance equals the one before it plus that row.
- The money in and out add up to the totals the statement prints.
- The opening balance plus every row equals the closing balance.
- Every row is dated inside the statement period.

If a check fails, nothing is imported, and you see the first row that doesn't fit. A
statement with no running balance and no totals that match its rows can't be checked, so
it isn't imported either. No model reads your amounts. Code reads every number, as it does
for the entries you type.

Credit card statements print what you owe instead of a balance, and a payment shows as a
negative amount. Goatside reads them that way when you import into a credit card account.

## Reviewing the rows

The review sorts the rows into groups:

- **Maybe logged** rows are close to an entry you typed but not exact: ₱180 typed for a
  ₱185 charge, a date up to a week off, or the right amount filed under another account
  (entries that name no account go to your default). Choose **Same entry** to link them. The entry takes the statement's amount and account. Otherwise the row is added as new.
- **New** rows get filed like typed entries, with a category from the agent. Rows that
  read the same, like 30 "Qr Payment" lines, get one question between them, and your
  answer files them all. Untick any you don't want. A row that looks like a transfer, such as "Outbound interbank transfer",
  has a menu to make it one.
- **Transfers** are money moving between your own accounts. A card payment, an ATM
  withdrawal or a row that names another of your accounts lands here, with the other
  account picked. If you already logged the other side, like the bank's transfer out
  for a card payment, the two become one transfer.
- **Already logged** rows match an entry you typed: same amount, within two days. They're
  linked, not added again. Choose **Not the same** if the match is wrong.
- Rows from a statement you imported before are left alone, so overlapping statements
  never add a row twice. That includes rows you deleted after importing: they stay
  deleted.

Imported rows don't create IOUs. "Transfer to Nini" on a bank statement could be a loan,
a gift or a bill, so file it with Nini by hand if it's a loan.

## After importing

If the statement ends at a different balance than Goatside has, you can set the account
to the statement's balance, or for a card to what you owe. That keeps your entries and moves
the starting balance, like **Correct the balance**.

To take an import back, choose **Undo import** right after, or **Undo** next to it under
**Imported before**. Undo removes the rows it added. Entries it linked stay as they are.

## What it can read

- Text PDFs: statements you download from your bank's app or website. Scanned paper
  statements have no text to read.
- CSV exports with a date, details, amounts and a running balance. Numeric dates like
  `09/12/2026` are read month first unless a day over 12 shows otherwise.

Built against GoTyme deposit account and Maya credit card statements. Other banks work
when their statements print a running balance or totals.
