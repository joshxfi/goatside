---
title: What leaves your instance
description: Where your ledger lives, and exactly what is sent to AI models.
---

Your ledger lives in your own Turso database. Goatside has no server of its own and
collects nothing: no analytics, no telemetry, no account with us.

Goatside sends two things out, both through your own Vercel AI Gateway:

| When              | What goes out                                                                                                                               | Model                                               |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| You log an entry  | The entry's text and amount, plus your category names with a few recent entries in each, and the people and accounts the line might involve | TypeSafe's Jev, `typesafe-ai/jev`                   |
| You ask the agent | Your question, the recent chat, and the ledger rows its tools look up to answer                                                             | DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash` |

Nothing goes out when you only browse your dashboard, edit an entry by hand or settle up
with someone.

## Running without AI

AI is optional. Without AI Gateway credentials, Goatside still works:

- Entries save with their amount and description, uncategorized.
- An entry without a sign still gets an "in or out?" question.
- You can file categories, people and accounts by hand in the edit drawer.

The agent's chat is the one feature that needs a model.

## Choosing other models

You can swap either model for any model on AI Gateway with the `DECISION_MODEL` and
`CHAT_MODEL` variables. See [Environment variables](/docs/self-hosting/environment).
